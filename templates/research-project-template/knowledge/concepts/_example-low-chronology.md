# Low Chronology {#sec-concept-low-chronology}

## Definition

The *Low Chronology* is a chronological model proposed by Israel
Finkelstein that shifts the conventional dating of Iron Age strata in
the southern Levant downward by roughly 50–75 years.

## Context in research

The model has far-reaching consequences for the reconstruction of the
region's political history, especially the question of the historical
existence and extent of a united monarchy under David and Solomon in
the 10th century BCE.

## Critical perspectives

Main criticisms of the *Low Chronology*:

1. Tensions with certain radiocarbon datings
2. Selective application to specific sites
3. Alternative explanations for the stratigraphic record
