# Outline: Iron Age IIA Chronology — the Negev Fortress Case

## Thesis

Applied to the central Negev fortresses, the Iron IIA chronology debate is
primarily a **Level-4 disagreement — about which evidence has priority —
disguised as a Level-1 disagreement about which 14C measurements count.**

## Claim chain

The debate's disputants largely share their 14C measurements (S2), so the
disagreement cannot be evidential in the way its tone suggests. Mapping it onto
four levels — data selection, calibration, phase modelling, framework choice —
locates the real seat of the dispute at Level 4 (S3). The central Negev is the
test case for that diagnosis, because its own 14C dataset is too thin to
arbitrate anything and the debate there is transferred from the northern sites
(S4). If the diagnosis holds, a regional-variation reading becomes available in
which Cohen's 10th-c. Negev horizon and Finkelstein's 9th-c. northern shift are
not actually competing claims (S5–S6).
